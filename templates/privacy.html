Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Spendly handles information when you use our personal expense tracking service.

Data We Collect

We may collect account details such as your name, email address, and login credentials, along with the expense information you choose to record, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as browser details and activity logs.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve Spendly, display your expense history and summaries, authenticate your account, and respond to support requests.

We may also use limited technical information to protect the service, detect misuse, and troubleshoot problems. We do not use your personal expense records to provide financial advice.

Data Storage

Your information is stored using reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong password and keep your account credentials private.

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal or operational requirements. You may contact us about questions concerning your information.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, authentication, security, or error monitoring. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services on our behalf and are expected to handle it responsibly.

We do not sell your personal information or expense records to third parties.

Your Choices

You are responsible for the information you enter into Spendly. You may choose not to provide optional information, though some features may not work without required account details. Contact us if you want to ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or the service change. The updated version will be posted on this page, and your continued use of Spendly after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Spendly handles information, please contact the Spendly service administrator.
